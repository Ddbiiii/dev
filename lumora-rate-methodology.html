How the illustration is calculated

A transparent reading companion for the local interest calculator, including its assumptions and limits.

03 notes · no live submission

Read the inputs first

The calculator uses a principal amount and a term in months. Its illustrative annual rate is 3.20% for terms below 12 months, 4.10% from 12 months, and 4.60% from 24 months. These are demo constants, not available offers.

Follow one worked example

Rp 100,000,000 × 4.10% × 12 ÷ 12 gives Rp 4,100,000 of illustrative simple interest. The principal is not included in the interest result. Compounding, tax, withdrawals, fees and changing rates are excluded.

Compare like with like

The product cards illustrate separate concepts and do not form executable terms. A rate alone does not describe when funds can be accessed. Read the access column and maturity notes alongside any percentage.

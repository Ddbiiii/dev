Ask what the energy label measures

The renewable-energy page is an explanation of questions to ask, not a verified sourcing claim about a real utility.

  1. Definition

    Find out whether the claim refers to generation, procurement, matching or a particular customer product.

  2. Period and boundary

    A useful statement names the time window and the scope of energy covered by the claim.

  3. Evidence

    Look for a clear source and review method. The demo has no certificates, generation assets or audited environmental results.

Separate generation from consumption ↗

The solar concept distinguishes what is generated, used on site and exported. The example chart is not a feasibility assessment.

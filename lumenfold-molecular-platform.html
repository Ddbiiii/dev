Keep structure and function in conversation

The molecular platform concept connects a design hypothesis to an experimentally observable response.

Design hypothesis
Describe the molecular feature being changed and the functional consequence it is expected to produce.
Measurement
Use a readout appropriate to the question and document controls that can expose a misleading response.
Iteration
Carry failed hypotheses into the next design note. A change in the model should leave a readable reason.

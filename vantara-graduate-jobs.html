A first role should include a learning plan

The graduate examples describe entry routes, not live vacancies. Look for support as carefully as you inspect the title.

  • Mentoring

    Ask who reviews early work and how often a new team member can get guidance.

  • Scope

    A clear first project is more useful than an undefined expectation to contribute everywhere.

  • Evidence

    Coursework, independent projects and team exercises can show relevant thinking when their context is explained.

Show the work behind the finished piece ↗

A compact case study gives someone enough context to discuss your judgement. It can come from professional work, study or an independent project.

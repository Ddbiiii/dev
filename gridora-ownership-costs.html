Questions for the address
Initial costsAsk which transaction, professional and setup costs apply to the specific property and location. Obtain current figures from the responsible sources.
Recurring costsIdentify building charges, maintenance responsibilities, utilities and other continuing commitments. Understand which costs are fixed and which can change.
Unplanned workCondition and future maintenance deserve a separate discussion with qualified professionals. The demo calculator cannot model a full household budget or the costs of owning a particular property.

Bring the question to the viewing

A listing is a starting record. Keep what you can observe separate from what requires documents, professional review or a second visit. The fictional examples here are designed to make that distinction easier to see.
